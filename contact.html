Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on ordering, delivery, Telangana bonus registration, and seed quality guarantees.

You can place an order directly through our website booking form, by messaging our official WhatsApp helpline at +91 70131 35345, or by visiting our Shetpalle seed center in person. We provide express dispatch across all mandals in Nizamabad, Kamareddy, Nirmal, and neighboring districts in Telangana.
The Telangana Government approves 33 notified fine rice varieties for the ₹500/Qtl incentive. Ganga Agri Genetics supplies 100% certified breeder stock for the 7 top-performing varieties: BPT 5204 (Samba Masuri), RNR 15048 (Telangana Sona), HMT Sona, Jai Sri Ram, KNM 1638, WGL-44, and KNM 7715. All meet strict grain length (<6mm) and width (<2mm) standards.
When you purchase seeds from Ganga Agri Genetics, you receive an official invoice with lot and batch numbers. Provide this invoice along with your land passbook (Pattadar Passbook) and Aadhaar number to your village AEO during crop registration. After harvesting, deliver grain with moisture below 17% to the PPC center for direct bank bonus disbursement.
All our seed lots are pre-tested in our on-site germination chambers in Shetpalle. We guarantee a minimum of 96% to 98% germination and 100% genetic purity. Every bag is polymer coated with bio-fungicide protection and hermetically sealed against moisture.
