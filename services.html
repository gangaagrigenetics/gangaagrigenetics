4-Stage Quality Guarantee

Scientific Precision from Research Plot to Seed Bag

Every seed lot processed in Shetpalle passes through our strict four-tier laboratory protocol to guarantee peak vigor and field performance.

01

Genetic Purity Profiling

Breeder seed stock verification using rigorous grow-out tests (GOT) and physical seed trait analysis to ensure 100% true-to-type genetics.

  • Field isolation inspections
  • Controlled selfing & crossing
  • Off-type rogue elimination
02

Germination & Vigor Chamber

Standard and cold-stress germination testing conducted in climate-controlled chambers to guarantee 96-98% field emergence viability.

  • 96% to 98% minimum germination
  • Vigor index profiling
  • Root-shoot elongation check
03

Bio-Polymer Seed Treatment

Automated polymer film coating incorporating broad-spectrum bio-fungicides and micronutrients to shield emerging radicles from soil-borne pathogens.

  • Uniform color & film coating
  • Anti-fungal blast protection
  • Enhanced dust-free handling
04

Hermetic Moisture Packaging

High-barrier, triple-layer hermetic pouches protecting seeds against ambient moisture fluctuations, extending shelf-life and vigor for multiple seasons.

  • Moisture content strictly < 12%
  • Tamper-evident seal
  • Lot & batch traceability QR
