Monsoon Season

Kharif Season (Monsoon)

June – October

Primary sowing season synchronized with the South-West Monsoon. Best for high-yield grains and commercial cash crops.

Recommended Ganga Hybrids:
  • 🌾 Hybrid Paddy: Ganga Gold-99, Ganga BPT-5204
  • 🌽 Hybrid Maize: Ganga Surya Mahabali
  • 🌱 High-Yield Soybean: Ganga Shakti-335
Winter Season

Rabi Season (Yasangi)

October – March

Post-monsoon winter crop benefiting from clear sunshine and cooler night temperatures. Excellent grain luster.

Recommended Ganga Hybrids:
  • 🌾 Rabi Fine Paddy: Ganga Telangana Sona (RNR 15048)
  • 🌽 Winter Corn: Ganga Surya Maize
  • 🌼 Oilseed Mustard: Ganga Peeli Sarson
Summer Season

Summer Season (Zaid)

February – May

High solar radiation season requiring drought-hardy, heat-tolerant crops with quick duration cycles.

Recommended Ganga Hybrids:
  • 🌾 Hybrid Pearl Millet: Ganga Tejasvi (85 Days)
  • 🌾 White Sorghum: Ganga Mahaveer Jowar
