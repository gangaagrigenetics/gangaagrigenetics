# Security Policy — Ganga Agri Genetics

At **Ganga Agri Genetics**, we take the security, integrity, and privacy of our agricultural platform, farmer data, and digital services very seriously.

---

## Supported Versions

We provide security updates and patches for the following versions of the Ganga Agri Genetics web application:

| Version | Supported          | Status |
| ------- | ------------------ | ------ |
| 2.x.x   | :white_check_mark: | Active (Current Production Release) |
| 1.x.x   | :x:                | Deprecated |
| < 1.0   | :x:                | Unsupported |

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a potential security vulnerability, data privacy issue, or unexpected behavior within our web application or services, please report it responsibly:

### 1. Primary Reporting Channel
- **Security Email**: [support@gangaagrigenetics.com](mailto:support@gangaagrigenetics.com)
- **Subject Line**: `[SECURITY] Potential Vulnerability in Ganga Agri Genetics Web App`
- **Urgent WhatsApp Helpline**: `+91 7013135345` (Direct Developer & Operational Desk)

### 2. What to Include in Your Report
To help us triage and resolve the issue quickly, please provide:
- A detailed description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
- Step-by-step instructions or Proof of Concept (PoC) to reproduce the issue.
- The affected component, file, or URL (e.g., `index.html`, `app.js`, `sw.js`, or API endpoints).
- Any relevant logs, screenshots, or network traces.

### 3. Response Commitment & SLA
- **Initial Acknowledgement**: Within **24 to 48 hours** of receiving your report.
- **Triage & Assessment**: Within **3 to 5 business days**.
- **Fix & Deployment**: We will release a verified patch as soon as possible and notify you when the fix is deployed to production.

---

## Responsible Disclosure

We kindly request that you:
- Allow us a reasonable amount of time to remediate the vulnerability before publicly disclosing it.
- Avoid accessing, modifying, or deleting real farmer inquiries, customer contact numbers, or operational data.
- Refrain from performing Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks or automated high-rate scraping that degrades service for farmers.

Thank you for helping keep Ganga Agri Genetics safe and reliable for our farming community across Telangana and India!
