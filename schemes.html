Telangana Govt Incentive Policy

Maximize Your Profit with the Telangana ₹500/Qtl Sannalu Bonus

Ganga Agri Genetics supplies 100% certified breeder seeds for all 7 eligible fine-grain paddy varieties.

Official Policy Breakdown

Telangana State ₹500 / Quintal Fine Rice Incentive

The Government of Telangana provides an additional bonus of ₹500 per quintal directly over standard MSP to encourage fine grain paddy cultivation.

Grain Length < 6.0 mm

Strictly slender fine rice size certified by government procurement analyzers.

Grain Width < 2.0 mm

Fine caliper standard meeting premium table-quality Sannalu specifications.

Moisture Below 17%

Sun-dried grain moisture grade required at government procurement centers (PPCs).

Biometric AEO Registration

Verified farmer land passbook entry with Agricultural Extension Officer digital portal.

Step-by-Step Process

How to Qualify & Receive Your ₹500/Qtl Bonus

Follow these 3 simple steps from seed purchase to harvest procurement.

1

Buy Certified Breeder Seeds

Purchase approved Sannalu varieties (BPT 5204, RNR 15048, KNM 1638, etc.) from Ganga Agri Genetics, Shetpalle and keep your official tax invoice with batch/lot number.

2

Register on AEO Digital Portal

Submit your seed purchase invoice, Aadhaar, and land passbook details to your local Agriculture Extension Officer (AEO) during the crop booking window.

3

Harvest & Direct Bank Bonus

Deliver sun-dried grain (moisture strictly below 17%) to the government procurement center. Receive standard MSP + ₹500/Qtl bonus directly in your bank account.
