Farmer Help & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about our hybrid seed varieties, germination testing, and distribution.

Are Ganga Agri Genetics hybrid seeds officially certified in Telangana?

Yes, 100%. All hybrid seeds produced and marketed by Ganga Agri Genetics comply with Indian Minimum Seed Certification Standards (IMSCS). Every lot is tested for genetic purity, physical purity, and minimum 90-98% germination before dispatch.

How can I buy seeds from Ganga Agri Genetics if I am outside Shetpalle?

You can order directly by calling our phone helpline at +91 70131 35345 or messaging via WhatsApp. We arrange parcel dispatch and transport to all major towns and rural mandals across Telangana and neighboring districts.

What makes Ganga Agri Genetics hybrid paddy varieties superior for Telangana soils?

Our paddy hybrids are bred specifically for local Telangana soil profiles (black cotton and red sandy loams). They feature strong root penetration, high tillering (25-35 tillers per plant), robust resistance to Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB) and Neck Blast, and superior milling recovery with fine slender grain quality.

Do you provide free soil and agronomic advisory for farmers?

Yes! We offer comprehensive technical consultation to our farmers. You can send photos of your crop or soil symptoms over WhatsApp to receive tailored recommendations on micro-nutrients, water management, and pest control.
